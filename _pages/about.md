Hello! I am **Dong Hee Seo**. I graduated from Soonchunhyang University with an M.S. in ICT Convergence Engineering in 2025, where I was fortunate to be advised by **Prof. Seok Young Lee**. Prior to that, I received my B.S. in Electronic Engineering from the same university in 2023.

My research interests include **robotics** and **control theory**, particularly robust and adaptive control methods for robot manipulators.
